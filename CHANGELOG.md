# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — first public release (October 2026)

| Part | Version |
|---|---|
| Blackout theme | 1.2.0 |
| Tweaks | 1.1.0 |
| Home widgets | 1.0.0 |
| Instant Answer | 1.3.0 |
| F1 card | 1.3.0 |
| Quick answers | 1.1.0 |

- Blackout theme: true black, three accent colours, results position (left, centre left, centred).
- Tweaks: same-tab results without click logging, no ads banner or Private Search badge, ⋮ result menu (summary,
  Anonymous View, copy link, pin or hide a site), keyboard keys.
- Home widgets: clock, weather, shortcuts, notes, calendar, currency, timer, and a Formula 1 widget from the F1
  card; on/off, drag to reorder.
- Instant Answer: short AI answers above results and one-page summaries; off until turned on; OpenCode Go or a
  model on your computer.
- F1 card: session results with tyre strategy, standings, schedule and calendar; teams by colour, drivers by
  initials.
- Quick answers: timer, parcel tracking, colour, coin, dice, random number, currency, and translation searches handed to
  Startpage's own translator.
